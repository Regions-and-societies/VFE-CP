[h1]Regions and Societies: Vanilla Factions Expanded Patch[/h1]
Compatibility patch: world objects from the Vanilla Factions Expanded family are classified under [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666060]Regions and Societies[/url] territory rules.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]One typed rule for the family:[/b] every VFE moving base (VFE Core's MovingBase and its subclasses, including VFE Medieval 2's Merchant Guild) is recognised as a caravan rather than a territorial holding.
[*] [b]Moving bases are caravans:[/b] a VFE base rolling across the world map neither claims nor holds ground.
[*] [b]Nothing else to patch:[/b] the other VFE faction modules use vanilla settlements with modded defs, already governed by Regions and Societies core.
[/list]

The patch binds the 1.6 VEF assembly directly, so framework drift is a compile error rather than a silent misclassification. No settings of its own - installed means enabled.
